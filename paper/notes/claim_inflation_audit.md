# Claim-Inflation Audit: Sections I–IX

**Scope:** sentence-level review of the current `paper/main.tex` for broad effectiveness, novelty, calibration, and practical-benefit claims. The evidence map was not modified. The goal is to preserve the distinction between component/development evidence, frozen M8 evidence, exploratory observations, architectural verification, and N/A outcomes.

| Area | Potentially inflated claim or risk | Audit disposition and evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| I, Introduction | Dataset search services “improve discovery”; metadata standards “improve description and communication.” | External contextual claims have citations to Dataset Search, Croissant, and Data Cards. The paper does not infer recommendation suitability from discoverability or documentation. |
| I, Contributions | The integrated pipeline could be read as an algorithmic novelty claim. | Wording explicitly calls it an implementation/integration, disclaims component-level novelty, and limits evaluation to named settings. No new algorithm claim is made. |
| II, Related Work | Broad claims about the state of dataset search, documentation, data quality, and diversification. | Relevant external claims are cited. ## Edits made during this audit

- Replaced “These outcomes support the selector's behavior” with a directly observed statement that R3 removed the one development-corpus family duplicate.
- Clarified that the local-envelope component measurements were separate component/development runs and that M8 separately measured fixed-corpus recommendation; this avoids implying a single end-to-end measurement across all paths.
- Replaced a possibility/causal framing of the human-pilot result with the descriptive observation that Format B received higher mean ratings in this pilot.
- Clarified that the consistency audit is an M8 analysis of M7 scores against the M5-related rubric, rather than implying that the audit itself belongs to M7.
- Defined the M8 ablation's two-stage averaging and disclosed that four tasks returned two candidates after gating; this bounds interpretation of the utility change.
- Changed H3's table status from “Supported” to “Criterion met” to match the narrow, corpus-conditioned interpretation.

## Remaining scope limits

The audit does not independently reproduce experiment outputs. Quantitative statements remain tied to the existing experiment artifacts/evidence map and their stated scopes. The M7 resource discrepancy is explicitly unresolved in Section VIII; the text uses the requested `bff0767` values consistently. No claim-map entry was changed.
