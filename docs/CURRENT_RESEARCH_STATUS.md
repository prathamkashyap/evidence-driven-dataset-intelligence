# Current research status

This page is the current orientation to the completed **Phase I** research. It separates recorded observations from what those observations establish. Read the [Phase I reporting corrections](PHASE1_REPORTING_CORRECTIONS.md) for the evidence and current interpretation; earlier milestone notes and the [historical viva](FINAL_VIVA_EVIDENCE_MAP.md) and [report evidence maps](FINAL_REPORT_EVIDENCE_MAP.md) preserve their original wording and can conflict with that interpretation. The [current defense brief](DEFENSE_BRIEF_CURRENT.md) replaces those maps as viva guidance, not as historical records.

## What was studied

M0–M8 cover a research prototype: local resource budgets, canonical records, retrieval, source-attributed evidence, bounded fingerprints, heuristic utility, and family/diversity-aware selection. M2 is a small retrieval study; M3–M7 are development-corpus component studies; M8 evaluates a fixed pool of 24 records against eight structured tasks (192 pairs). M8 does not measure live catalogue recall. The implementation can record provenance and distinguish per-entry observation states from cross-entry resolution states, but agreement is not independent verification. Fingerprint observations apply only to the inspected sample. See the [project specification](PROJECT_SPEC.md), [M2 results](M2_RESULTS.md), [M7 results](M7_RESULTS.md), and [M8 summary at the Phase 9C baseline](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/ba7fe87b1aaeaf20512378d37f7c729b24443fab/experiments/m8/results/m8_summary.json).

The [approved ten-page Phase I paper](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/ba7fe87b1aaeaf20512378d37f7c729b24443fab/paper/final_submission/main.pdf) is the historical publication of record (PDF SHA-256 `21896eaeb05e497a7654f3c9fdcfb6811da36e3094cf01d5c3ed034487b7aa0c`). Its original statements are preserved; the [reporting corrections](PHASE1_REPORTING_CORRECTIONS.md) qualify them without replacing experimental records. The [research companion](https://evidence-driven-research-companion.vercel.app/) presents the implemented architecture, illustrative interactions and recorded Phase I results; it is not an independent relevance evaluation.

## What the recorded evidence means

- **M2 resources:** the 453.22 MiB RSS is the shared benchmark process, not an isolated MiniLM or BM25 measurement. Calls were ordered BM25, dense, then hybrid, without repeats or counterbalancing; they cannot establish a controlled dense-versus-hybrid speed advantage. [Results](../experiments/m2/results/benchmark.json) · [runner](../scripts/run_m2_benchmark.py)
- **M5 metrics:** historical nDCG used common corpus order rather than predicted ranking; the strict-rank correlation broke ties by identifier. Neither establishes comparative ranking effectiveness. Corrected metrics have not been computed. [Runner](../scripts/run_m5_utility.py) · [recorded results](../experiments/m5/results/baseline_ablation.json)
- **M8 selection:** macro internal heuristic utility progresses from 0.441350 for BM25/test-vector RRF to 0.818950 after gating, then 0.858825 for utility-only R1 and later stages. The cumulative gating contrast changes eligibility and realized set sizes; gated candidates receive zero utility. Equal aggregates do not imply identical rankings or sets. The numbers are not independent relevance or an isolated causal effect. [Frozen summary](../experiments/m8/results/m8_summary.json) · [corrections](PHASE1_REPORTING_CORRECTIONS.md#m8-utility-reference-labels-and-sensitivity)
- **Diversity and H1:** M8 H1 is N/A because no R1 set met its same-family trigger. M7's development example and the separate M8 Iris rung-7/rung-8 observation do not change that registered outcome. The diversity-weight sweep reached the selector and changed some sets; configured positive/risk weight perturbations did not reach the intended scorer, so their unchanged outputs do not establish weight robustness. [Sensitivity and selection](PHASE1_REPORTING_CORRECTIONS.md#m8-utility-reference-labels-and-sensitivity) · [M7](M7_RESULTS.md)
- **Relevance and calibration:** M8 reference labels are project-authored. Brier and ECE report internal score–rubric consistency, not independent relevance or established probability calibration. The exploratory card pilot has three participants: all used the same initial schedule, one was an author, and repeat linkage is uncertain for two. Format B supplied explanations absent from A, while the understanding question asks about those explanations; the effect is not isolated. [Pilot findings](M8_RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md) · [instrument](../data/benchmarks/m8_human_eval_instrument.md)
- **Timing and replay:** 327.49 ms is the timed eight-task M8 harness interval, including input loading and evaluation, excluding startup, initial preflight and output serialization; 33.11 MiB is that process's lifetime peak RSS. The offline input policy and a socket-blocking test during construction/input loading do not establish monitored whole-run egress. M8 used a 64-bin `TestDenseEncoder`, not M2 MiniLM; the application seed is known but historical `PYTHONHASHSEED` is not, so exact fresh-process dense/hybrid ordering is unestablished. [Corrections and code links](PHASE1_REPORTING_CORRECTIONS.md#m8-harness-timing-offline-policy-and-replay)

## Boundaries and next research

Frozen results and the [benchmark manifest](../data/benchmarks/m8_benchmark_manifest.json) are historical evidence, while the repository's active software is an implementation that may evolve; present-day code must not silently be substituted for the recorded run. The M8 evaluation document is a [historical, hash-contracted protocol](M8_EVALUATION.md), not a current execution authorization.

Independent suitability judgments, gate accuracy on independently adjudicated cases, general user benefit, downstream outcomes, repeatable resource measurements and broader live-catalogue performance remain unvalidated. The [Phase II benchmark](phase2/README.md) is a proposed design, not a completed study. Formal protocol freeze, human calibration and data collection are not authorized by this documentation; Gate B remains closed.
