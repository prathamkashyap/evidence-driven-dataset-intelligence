# Phase I reporting corrections

This note clarifies the interpretation of the frozen Phase I results. The [approved 10-page manuscript](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/paper/final_submission/main.pdf) remains the historical publication of record. These reporting corrections do not modify the original experimental artifacts or supersede that manuscript.

## M2 retrieval and resource measurements

M2 used `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` for dense retrieval. The configured model revision was `main`, and the exact resolved weight revision is unavailable in the archived run record. M2 measured four development queries with a single call per method per query, always in BM25 → dense → hybrid order. The hybrid call performs both component retrievals and reciprocal rank fusion (RRF). Earlier calls may have affected the process state seen by later calls. The study did not repeat or counterbalance the calls, so the observed times do not establish a controlled comparison of dense and hybrid speed or the cause of their difference. [Model configuration](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/configs/m2_retrieval.json#L5-L8) · [Runner](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/scripts/run_m2_benchmark.py#L17-L28) · [Hybrid call](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/retrieval/engine.py#L23-L32)

The recorded 453.22 MiB peak RSS (475,234,304 bytes) is for the shared process that built the engine and ran all three methods. Individual backend peaks, including a BM25-only peak, were not measured. [Frozen M2 benchmark](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/experiments/m2/results/benchmark.json#L46-L49)

## M5 historical ranking metrics

M5 evaluated three utility configurations on four tasks and ten records using a project-authored rubric. The nDCG implementation read reference gains in the input corpus order, which was the same for all three configurations. It did not sort by predicted utility. The historical correlation calculation sorted by predicted score and reference grade separately, breaking ties on both sides by dataset identifier before applying the strict-rank formula. Its values describe those specific orderings. The recorded nDCG and correlation values do not establish comparative ranking effectiveness or a gain from adding evidence and fingerprints. Corrected metrics and average-tied-rank Spearman estimates have not been computed for this report. [Prediction construction](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/scripts/run_m5_utility.py#L130-L148) · [Metric calls](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/scripts/run_m5_utility.py#L190-L203) · [Metric implementation](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/scripts/run_m5_utility.py#L35-L64) · [Frozen M5 results](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/experiments/m5/results/baseline_ablation.json)

## M8 harness timing, offline policy, and replay

**Timing and memory.** The 327.49 ms interval covered the complete timed eight-task evaluation harness: loading and validating frozen inputs, baselines, ablations, hypotheses, sensitivity, robustness, and the score-consistency audit. Process startup, harness configuration construction, initial runner preflight, and output serialization were outside the timer. Recommendation-only latency was not measured. The 33.11 MiB figure is the process-lifetime peak RSS. The 20 s per-task budget is a design target with a different measurement scope. [Harness timer](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/evaluation/harness.py#L306-L336) · [Runner boundary](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/scripts/run_m8_benchmark.py#L35-L59) · [Recorded resources](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/experiments/m8/results/m8_summary.json#L3470-L3494)

**Network.** The configuration forbids network egress and uses frozen local inputs. The resource record assigns `zero_egress_verified: true` unconditionally. The socket test intercepts `socket.socket.connect` during harness construction and input loading. The available evidence therefore establishes the offline design and the scope of the input-loading test. Whole-run network telemetry is unavailable. [Offline policy](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/configs/m8_evaluation.json#L3-L8) · [Flag assignment](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/evaluation/resources.py#L19-L32) · [Socket test](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/tests/test_m8_evaluation.py#L98-L106)

**Dense ordering and exact replay.** M8's dense ablation used `TestDenseEncoder`, a 64-bin token-count representation, and its hybrid condition fused that ordering with BM25. These orderings were produced within a fixed pool. The encoder uses Python's built-in string `hash()`. The application seed `20260902` is recorded, while the historical `PYTHONHASHSEED` is unknown. Exact fresh-process replay of the dense and hybrid orderings is therefore unestablished. [Ablation implementation](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/evaluation/ablations.py#L258-L306) · [Test encoder](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/retrieval/dense.py#L10-L27) · [Run metadata](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/experiments/m8/results/m8_summary.json#L3470-L3494)

## M8 utility, reference labels, and sensitivity

The cumulative ablation measures the system's internal heuristic utility on selected items. Eligibility and realized set size change at the gating step. Its results describe this frozen candidate pool and utility definition. M8's task-compatibility reference labels were authored within the project. Brier and ECE describe score–rubric consistency and cannot establish independent relevance or probability calibration. [Ablation result construction](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/evaluation/ablations.py#L338-L355) · [M8 rubric](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/data/benchmarks/m8_rubric.md) · [Consistency audit](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/evaluation/consistency.py#L47-L96)

The positive-weight (±20%) and risk-weight sensitivity routines set `scoring_weights` in the engine configuration, but the recommendation scoring calls omit those weights and use the defaults. The archived unchanged rankings therefore provide no evidence of robustness to changes in those scoring weights. The separate `lambda_div` sweep passes its setting to the selector, where it changes the marginal gain used for selection. It remains a recorded sensitivity result for this pool. [Sensitivity setup](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/evaluation/sensitivity.py#L73-L181) · [Recommendation call sites](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/ranking/pipeline.py#L145-L166) · [Selector](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/ranking/diversification.py#L65-L77) · [Scorer defaults](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/src/dataset_intelligence/ranking/scoring.py#L23-L44) · [Frozen M8 sensitivity results](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/experiments/m8/results/m8_summary.json#L3650)

## Exploratory card-format pilot

Three participants each completed seven primary paired comparisons of a minimal card (A) and an evidence-rich card (B) for the same task–dataset case. The recorded first-exposure mean differences favored B and are descriptive. All participants used the same schedule (five B-first and two A-first pairs), one participant was a project author, and repeat-exposure linkage is uncertain for two participants. The Understanding item asks why a dataset was surfaced and how it fits the task, information the B format supplies directly. The design does not isolate the effects of format, question wording, and presentation order or establish general preference or recommendation correctness. [Study and aggregate ratings](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/docs/M8_RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md#L18-L66) · [Disclosed limitations](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/docs/M8_RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md#L116-L126) · [Questionnaire item](https://github.com/prathamkashyap/evidence-driven-dataset-intelligence/blob/cb829a97d5f65f9389c0d6599765942a43f2ddbe/data/benchmarks/m8_human_eval_instrument.md#L37-L38)
