01 THE PROBLEM

A match in text
isn’t a match for your task.

Catalogs mix publisher claims, incomplete metadata, and observations made on small samples. A search result can look relevant and still fail a confirmed requirement.

This research moves the decision from “Does the description sound right?” to “What makes this dataset suitable, what evidence supports that, and what remains unknown?”

Hard constraints are checked before ranking.
