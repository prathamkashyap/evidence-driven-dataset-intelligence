# M3 Experiments — Evidence Acquisition and Evidence Ledger

## Overview

This directory contains empirical artifacts generated by Milestone 3 (`scripts/run_m3_ledger.py`) executing over the 10 canonical dataset records from M1 (`experiments/m1/results/development_corpus.jsonl`).

## Scope & Disclaimers

1. **Candidate evidence, not recommendation quality:** M3 answers what evidence exists, its provenance, and whether claims agree or conflict. It does not rank datasets or claim one dataset is superior.
2. **Immutable ledger:** `results/evidence_ledger.jsonl` contains immutable, content-addressed `LedgerEntry` records. Evidence IDs are deterministic and independent of observation timestamps.
3. **Non-numerical resolution:** `results/resolution_summary.json` categorizes claims into `corroborated`, `conflicting`, `unresolved`, and `unknown` without computing confidence weights, belief masses, or suitability scores.
4. **Bounded content probes:** Content observations inspect at most 32 records under M0 empirical resource caps; they do not represent whole-dataset audits.
5. **Kaggle policy:** Kaggle records are metadata-only under the measured M0 policy; sample probes are explicitly recorded as `unsupported`.

## Generated Artifacts

- `results/evidence_ledger.jsonl`: 107 immutable evidence entries spanning metadata claims, documentation claims, Croissant claims, access probes, and bounded content observations.
- `results/resolution_summary.json`: Detailed non-numerical resolution summary and runtime resource metrics (23.47 MiB peak RSS, well within the 128 MiB ceiling).
