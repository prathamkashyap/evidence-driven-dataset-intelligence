# M6 Experiments — Popularity-Bias Audit & Long-Tail Exploration Simulation

## Overview

This directory contains empirical artifacts generated by Milestone 6 (`scripts/run_m6_exploration.py`) evaluating popularity profiling, exposure disparity, conservative family linkage, and under-exposed exploration mechanics across the 10-dataset development corpus.

## Scope & Methodological Disclaimers

1. **Development Simulation, Not Web-Scale Discovery:** The local candidate corpus contains 10 datasets. Candidates outside M2's top-$k$ that are selected into an exploration pool are **under-exposed local candidates**, not newly discovered web datasets.
2. **Platform Opacity & Under-Observed Popularity:** Only Hugging Face (4 datasets) and Kaggle (2 datasets) expose native popularity counters in M1. OpenML (2 datasets) and UCI (2 datasets) have no native popularity counters and are classified as `under_observed` with stratum `unknown`.
3. **Unknown Popularity $\neq$ Low Popularity:** Datasets with `unknown` popularity are **never** labeled "long-tail" or "hidden gems". They are categorized as *under-observed exploration candidates*.
4. **M5 Utility Independence:** M5 task utility scores are strictly independent of popularity profiling ($U_{\text{M6}} \equiv U_{\text{M5}}$).

## Generated Artifacts

- `results/popularity_profiles.jsonl`: 10 source-aware `DatasetPopularityProfile` records (5 measured, 5 under_observed).
- `results/popularity_audit.json`: Guarded exposure disparity metrics ($ES$, $EDR$, $UWE$, $\bar{U}$) across the 4 development queries.
- `results/exploration_pools.jsonl`: Dual-pool exploration outputs per query.
- `results/m6_summary.json`: Summary statistics, under-observed yields, and runtime resource measurements (25.08 MiB peak RSS, well below 128 MiB ceiling).
